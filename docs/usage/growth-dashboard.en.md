# Growth dashboard

[中文](growth-dashboard.md) · [Documentation index](../README.md)

Select a person on the TV **Archives** page, under All domains or Health. Separate height and weight cards show the latest measurement, unit, date, trend and three most recent measurements. The dashboard works for Abu and other attributed people without a predefined roster.

- Ranges: the last 3 calendar months, last year and all stages; all stages is the default.
- The X axis follows actual measurement dates; the Y axis shows numeric ticks and units. Height and weight have separate scales.
- Submit measurements with explicit dates, values and units through the existing IM or archive intake. Attributed records authorized for TV display update with archive verification. Supported fields are `height`, 身高, 身长, `weight` and 体重.
- Different units and samples remain separate; the browser does not convert, sum or combine them into one curve. Exact measurements retain their original decimal strings.
- Unclear dates, units or values are not manufactured into points. Pending confirmation, response limits and unavailable statistics are disclosed. Select a shorter range to narrow an oversized query.
- The current public person API exposes no verifiable birth date, so the axis uses calendar dates. The dashboard does not infer age from names or senders or bypass identity-field visibility. Age/month-age labels require a future connection to an authorized, confirmed birth date.

Use left/right remote keys to move between range buttons and confirm to apply a range. Move right from the last button to the reading region; use up/down to read the content. Milk charts (day/week/month) and other existing archive curves also show X/Y ticks.

Curves show recorded measurements; they do not predict future height/weight or provide medical percentile judgments. Data covers the authorized analysis results independently of the current archive-card page.
