# Documentation language / 文档语言

- `README.md` 以中文为主，`README.en.md` 提供完整英文对应内容，彼此保留语言切换链接。
- 新增或实质改写的对外项目介绍、使用和部署文档优先提供中英文。独立译本使用 `.en.md`；简短导航可在同一文件内并列中英文。
- 技术事实、命令、版本和交付状态必须在两种语言中同步更新；明确区分已实现能力、历史验证与后续计划。
- 历史证据、内部实施记录和现有设计可保留原语言，实质改写时再补对应版本。
- 公开示例仅使用合成资料和占位路径；真实家庭档案、凭据、签名私钥及未脱敏截图不进入文档或仓库。

- Keep `README.md` primarily in Chinese and maintain a complete `README.en.md`, with reciprocal language links.
- Prefer Chinese and English for new or substantially rewritten public-facing project, usage and deployment documentation. Use `.en.md` for separate translations; short navigation pages may include both languages.
- Update technical facts, commands, versions and delivery status together in both languages. Distinguish implemented capabilities, historical validation and future plans.
- Historical evidence, internal implementation records and existing designs may retain their original language until substantially rewritten.
- Public examples use synthetic material and placeholder paths. Never include real household archives, credentials, signing private keys or unredacted screenshots in documentation or source control.
